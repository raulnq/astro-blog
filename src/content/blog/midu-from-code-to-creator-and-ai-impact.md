Midu, a prominent Spanish-speaking streamer and former Lead Frontend Architect at a multinational, shares his unique trajectory from accidental programmer to influential content creator. His early academic journey was marked by significant struggles in computer science, nearly leading to expulsion, before a personal resolve ignited a profound passion for web performance optimization. This drive to improve and make an impact on users, often working late nights, defined his early career across various tech roles including a pivotal move from a consultant to a Norwegian startup and ultimately a multinational. This rich experience culminated in his decision to transition to full-time content creation, driven by a desire to provide high-quality, experience-based programming education in Spanish, a resource he lacked during his formative years.

Addressing skepticism regarding the "professional" nature of programming as a content creator, Midu asserts his 18 years of real-world development experience, including contributions to Firefox OS and JavaScript APIs. He highlights his current projects, such as "Adevents" (a programming challenge platform with 3 million monthly visits) and the official website for Ibai Llanos' "La Velada del Año" (handling 1.5 million visits in two days), as tangible demonstrations of complex engineering challenges solved through his content. He posits a controversial view on the "irrelevance" of code, arguing that code is merely a means to solve user problems, a concept historically accepted with frameworks and libraries. For Midu, the true value lies in problem-solving and delivering user-centric outcomes, rather than the code itself, though good practices remain crucial for maintainability.

Discussing the disruptive influence of Artificial Intelligence, Midu adopts a pragmatic stance, welcoming "vibe coders" who leverage AI to solve problems efficiently. He contends that while AI automates coding, it necessitates deeper involvement in business logic and product strategy from developers, shifting their role towards leadership and process optimization. Citing Jevons Paradox, he observes that AI's accessibility fuels increased demand for digital transformation, creating new opportunities. While acknowledging job displacement in some sectors, particularly for large, non-reinventing consultancies, he foresees a broader "scattering" of developer roles. Ultimately, Midu stresses that intellectual curiosity remains the paramount trait for engineers to navigate this evolving landscape, enabling them to understand underlying mechanisms and truly harness AI's super powers, thus bridging the gap between new and experienced professionals.
